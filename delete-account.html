Delete Your SecureFox Account

This page explains how to request deletion of your SecureFox VPN account and the data associated with it. SecureFox is developed and operated by SecureFox.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to info@securefox.net with the subject "Account Deletion Request".
  2. Send the email from the same email address you use to sign in to SecureFox (your Google account email). This lets us verify that the account belongs to you.
  3. We will delete your account and associated data within 30 days and confirm by email once it is done.

What data is deleted

Data Purpose Action
Account email address Authentication Permanently deleted
Subscription status and purchase identifiers Verifying your Premium subscription Permanently deleted
Device identifier Session and connection management Permanently deleted

What we do not store

SecureFox does not log or store your browsing activity, connection timestamps, IP addresses, or traffic data, so there is nothing further to delete.

Data retention

Once your account is deleted, the data listed above is removed from our systems. We may retain limited records only where required by law (for example, payment records held by Google Play).

Important: cancel your subscription

Deleting your account does not cancel an active subscription automatically. To avoid further charges, cancel it in Google Play: Play Store > Profile > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions > SecureFox > Cancel.
